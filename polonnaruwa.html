About Polonnaruwa

Polonnaruwa was the thriving royal capital of Sri Lanka during the 11th–13th centuries. It features well-preserved monumental ruins, grand audience halls, and ancient artificial lakes built by King Parakramabahu the Great.

Top Highlights

  • Gal Viharaya: Four magnificent rock-carved Buddha figures in seated, standing, and reclining poses.
  • Royal Palace of Parakramabahu: A multi-storied stone fortress ruin.
  • Polonnaruwa Vatadage: A beautifully carved circular relic house.
  • Parakrama Samudra: A vast ancient reservoir sustaining agriculture for centuries.
