Golden palm-fringed bays, blue whale watching excursions, and stunning sunset vantage points.

About Mirissa

Mirissa is one of the top beach destinations on Sri Lanka's south coast. Famous for its relaxed vibe, surfing breaks, seafood restaurants, and as one of the best locations in the world for spotting Blue Whales in the wild.

Top Highlights

  • Coconut Tree Hill: Iconic palm grove dome overlooking the Indian Ocean.
  • Blue Whale Safari: Morning boat expeditions to view the world's largest mammals.
  • Parrot Rock: A small tidal island offering panoramic views of Mirissa Bay.
  • Secret Beach: A hidden tropical cove perfect for swimming and relaxation.
