14 Days / 13 Nights Package

Scenic Hill Country & Udawalawe Safari

A relaxed, leisure-paced journey through Negombo, Sigiriya, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya train ride, Udawalawe elephant safari, and Galle fort beach resort.

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Days 1 & 2

Negombo Arrival & Sigiriya Transfer

Airport greeting and private transfer to Negombo. The following day, travel through lush tropical countryside to Sigiriya for leisure surrounded by nature.

Days 3 & 4

Sigiriya Leisure & Cultural Excursions

Leisure days with your dedicated vehicle and chauffeur at disposal for visiting Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress, Dambulla Cave Temple, or traditional village tours.

Days 5 & 6

Kandy Hill Capital Stay

Transfer to Kandy, the cultural capital of Sri Lanka. Visit Kandy town, the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic, and enjoy scenic mountain city viewpoints.

Days 7 & 8

Scenic Train to Nuwara Eliya Tea Country

Take the world-famous scenic train ride from Kandy to Nanu Oya. Relax among Nuwara Eliya's misty tea plantations, waterfalls, and colonial gardens.

Days 9 - 11

Udawalawe Elephant Safari Stay

Travel south to Udawalawe. Experience 4x4 Jeep Safaris at Udawalawe National Park, world-renowned for its dense wild elephant herds and wildlife sanctuary.

Days 12 - 14

Galle Beach Resort & Airport Departure

Transfer to coastal Galle. Explore UNESCO Galle Dutch Fort before spending relaxing beach days at your resort prior to airport departure.
