5 Days / 4 Nights Package

Hill Country & Train Experience

Discover Kandy Temple of the Tooth, take the famous scenic blue train journey to Nuwara Eliya tea estates, and explore Ella Nine Arch Bridge.

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival & Kandy Royal Capital Transfer

Pickup from Colombo airport or hotel. Drive to Kandy with stops at Pinnawala Elephant Sanctuary and spice gardens. Evening visit to the Temple of the Tooth Relic.

Day 2

Scenic Blue Train to Nuwara Eliya

Board the legendary mountain train from Kandy to Nanu Oya. Enjoy magnificent tea estate panoramas. Explore Nuwara Eliya Gregory Lake and colonial gardens.

Day 3

Nuwara Eliya Tea Factory & Ella Excursion

Tour a working Ceylon tea factory and tea leaves plantation. Drive through Ella gap, visit Nine Arch Bridge, and hike Little Adam's Peak.

Day 4 & 5

Ravana Waterfalls & Coastal Airport Transfer

Visit Ravana Falls and Buduruwagala rock carvings before heading back down to Colombo/Airport via southern expressway.
