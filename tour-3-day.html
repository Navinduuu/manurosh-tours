3 Days / 2 Nights Package

Wildlife & Beach Express Tour

A quick southern getaway featuring Yala leopard safari, Kosgoda turtle conservation project, Galle Fort, and Hikkaduwa beach relaxation.

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Transfer to Yala National Park

Pickup from Colombo Airport or hotel. Direct highway transfer down to Yala. Afternoon relaxing by pool or optional evening coastal safari drive.

Day 2

Yala Morning Safari & Galle Dutch Fort

Dawn 4x4 Jeep Safari at Yala National Park for wildlife tracking. Drive along the southern coastline to UNESCO Galle Dutch Fort for a sunset walk.

Day 3

Hikkaduwa Beach & Turtle Hatchery Departure

Relax at Hikkaduwa beachfront resort. Visit Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery project en route to airport for evening departure.
