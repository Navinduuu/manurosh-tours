16 Days / 15 Nights Package

Grand Island Discovery Tour

Comprehensive island journey covering Negombo, Yapahuwa, Anuradhapura, Trincomalee beach, Sigiriya, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya train, Yala safari, and Hikkaduwa.

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Airport Greeting & Negombo Arrival

Meet and assist on arrival at Colombo International Airport by your private chauffeur guide. Transfer to coastal Negombo and enjoy a city tour visiting Negombo Lagoon, fish market, and Dutch Fort.

Days 2 & 3

Yapahuwa Citadel & Ancient Anuradhapura

Visit Yapahuwa medieval rock fortress. Proceed to Anuradhapura, the oldest ancient civilization in Sri Lanka. Tour the sacred Sri Maha Bodhi tree, Ruwanweliseya Stupa, and Mihintale rock temple.

Days 4 & 5

Coastal Beach Leisure in Trincomalee

Drive to Trincomalee on the east coast for complete beach relaxation at your resort. Optional boat trip to Pigeon Island Marine National Park for snorkeling with sea turtles.

Days 6 & 7

Hiriwadunna Village, Sigiriya Rock & Polonnaruwa

Experience local village life with a bullock cart ride in Hiriwadunna. Climb the world-famous 5th-century Sigiriya Lion Rock Citadel and explore Polonnaruwa medieval ruins by bicycle.

Days 8 - 10

Matale Spice Garden & Kandy Cultural Capital

Visit Matale spice garden. Watch the Kandy Cultural Dance performance, visit the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic, drive around Kandy Lake, and explore Royal Peradeniya Botanical Gardens.

Days 11 & 12

Hill Country Scenic Train, Ella & Yala Safari

Board the iconic hill country train to Nanu Oya. Visit Ella Nine Arch Bridge and Little Adam's Peak, then embark on a 4x4 Jeep Safari at Yala National Park for leopards and elephants.

Days 13 - 16

Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery & Hikkaduwa Beach Stay

Visit Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery. Spend remaining days relaxing at Hikkaduwa beachfront resort before private airport departure transfer.
