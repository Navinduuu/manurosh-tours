Kandy One Day Excursion

Explore Sri Lanka's cultural capital, ancient temples, spice gardens, and elephant sanctuaries in a single day.

Excursion Highlights

Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage

Watch Asian elephants bathing and feeding along the riverbanks in a protected wildlife environment.

Herbal & Spice Plantation

Guided walk discovering native cinnamon, cardamom, nutmeg, and traditional Sri Lankan Ayurvedic herbs.

Giragama Tea Factory & Plantation

Experience authentic Ceylon tea processing from leaf picking to tasting world-famous teas.

Peradeniya Royal Botanical Gardens

Stroll under giant orchid houses, royal palm avenues, and century-old tropical flora collections.

Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Dalada Maligawa)

Visit Sri Lanka's most sacred Buddhist temple housing the sacred tooth relic of Lord Buddha.
