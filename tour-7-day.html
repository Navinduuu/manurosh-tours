7 Days / 6 Nights Package

Cultural Triangle & Wildlife Safari

Explore UNESCO ancient capitals, climb Sigiriya Rock Citadel, tour Matale spice gardens, visit Kandy, and go on a wild leopard safari at Yala National Park.

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Airport Arrival & Negombo Beach

Warm welcome at Colombo International Airport by your private chauffeur guide. Transfer to Negombo and relax on the beach or enjoy a boat ride along Dutch Canal.

Day 2

Anuradhapura Sacred Ancient City

Drive to Anuradhapura. Explore the ruins of Sri Lanka's ancient kingdom including Sri Maha Bodhi tree, ancient stupas, and royal palaces.

Day 3

Sigiriya Rock Fortress & Polonnaruwa

Climb the magnificent 5th-century Sigiriya Rock Fortress in the morning. Afternoon excursion to Polonnaruwa ancient stone monuments and Gal Viharaya Buddha statues.

Day 4

Dambulla Cave Temple & Kandy

Visit Dambulla Golden Rock Cave Temple with thousands of ancient Buddha paintings. Stop at Matale Spice Garden en route to Kandy for a cultural dance show.

Day 5

Kandy to Yala National Park

Visit the Temple of the Tooth Relic in Kandy. Drive south through hill country passes down to Yala for evening wildlife preparation.

Day 6 & 7

Yala Wildlife Safari & Airport Departure

Early morning 4x4 Jeep Safari in Yala National Park to spot wild elephants, sloth bears, and leopards. Return via coastal highway for evening airport transfer.
